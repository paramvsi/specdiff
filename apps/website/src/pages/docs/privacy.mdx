## What happens to your files

Files are read and compared in your browser, in background workers. Open the network panel while you compare: the spec is not sent anywhere.

When you load a URL, your browser fetches it directly. If the server does not allow cross-origin requests, the load fails and Specdiff explains why; Specdiff runs no proxy. See [FAQ: CORS](/docs/faq/#why-did-loading-a-url-fail).

## What is stored

History, saved sources and settings are kept in your browser’s IndexedDB. You can delete single entries or clear everything from History and Settings. Nothing is synced.

## Share links

A share link carries the files after the `#`. Browsers never send that part of a URL to a server. Lock it with a passphrase and the content is encrypted in your browser (AES-GCM, key from PBKDF2); the passphrase never leaves it. See [Share links](/docs/share-links/).

## AI

Specdiff makes no AI calls. “Copy for LLM” copies text to your clipboard; what you do with it is up to you.

<Callout>The website uses no cookies and does not track file content.</Callout>
