## What is in the link

The files are compressed into the URL after the `#`. Browsers never send that part to a server. You can include the rules, your review decisions and the change that is open.

```text
https://…/app/#v=1&c=<compressed comparison>
https://…/app/#v=1&e=1&s=<salt>&i=<iv>&c=<encrypted comparison>
```

The comparison is JSON, compressed with raw DEFLATE and encoded as base64url.

## Lock it

Add a passphrase and the content is encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM; the key comes from the passphrase through PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 310,000 iterations). The passphrase is never stored or sent. Whoever opens the link sees an unlock screen.

## Large comparisons

Above about 8 KB of URL, Specdiff warns you and offers a `comparison.specdiff.json` file to download instead. Drop that file into the app to open it.

<Callout type="info">Share links open in the app. A link that lands on the website’s 404 page has lost its `/app/` part.</Callout>
