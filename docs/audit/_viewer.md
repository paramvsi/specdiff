### Top viewer differences (by severity)
1. **The app's phone layouts (M-01/M-02/M-03) render the full desktop top bar and overflow horizontally (485 px document at 390 px).** It breaks the phone layout outright and is likely a WCAG 1.4.10 reflow failure.
2. **What changed, filtered to "my endpoints" (V-04), doesn't actually narrow the list:** 22 rows instead of the 5 chosen-endpoint changes, unsorted by severity, unused nav entries not dimmed, and checklist lines clipped.
3. **Breaking count disagreement:** the Viewer shows "Breaking 5" / "5 breaking" for the same comparison the app shows as 4 (B-2).
4. **M-04 has no Try it** although the approved frame shows it. This needs an owner ruling.
5. **The Viewer root doesn't fill the viewport,** leaving a white band under every short page. It's very visible in dark mode and was measured in the harness; embeds will inherit it [inferred].
6. **The version-failed state (V-01 error) shows full content and resets "compared with" to "Nothing".** The design shows skeletons and keeps 1.5.0.
7. **Search and dialog chrome differ:** an extra title row, a blue focus box, and no footer band in Search; Auth and My-endpoints dialogs have no header divider or footer band and are centred rather than top-anchored.
8. **Copy defects:** raw Markdown backticks on M-03, "Deprecated. / Deprecated." stutter, "PaymentStatus in PaymentStatus", "1 calls", "0.0 KB", and producer advice in consumer-facing Viewer text.
9. **Docs/History is a segmented pill control on every endpoint frame;** the design uses underline tabs.
10. **Code samples aren't wrapped or highlighted, and the chosen language doesn't move to the first tab.**
