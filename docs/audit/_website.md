### Top website differences (by severity)

1. **Rule detail pages show unfilled templates and generic copy** (W04-RuleDetail, -390): "Example message" renders `‹operation›` / `‹target›` placeholders on 71 of 76 rule pages. The headline, intro, "Who it breaks" and "How to avoid it" are one-line generic strings, not the designed copy, and the before/after panes show the whole generic `/a` fixture instead of a focused excerpt.
2. **Rules table on phones is broken** (W03-Rules-390): the Rule column is about 70 px, so rule IDs split mid-word across up to 6 lines.
3. **The docs severity cards hide their symbols** (W02-Docs, -390): the `! ? + ¶` glyphs are grey on a solid crimson, amber, green or grey disc, so severity is shown by colour alone (CLAUDE.md rule 4, a11y).
4. **The hero `npx` command is truncated** on every Home v2 frame ("npx specdiff-cli old.yaml ne…"), and on phone the terminal cursor overlaps the command.
5. The Home v2 dark "spec stays in the tab" tile loses its brand tint; the CTA panel is inset instead of near full-bleed.
6. Site-wide: an extra theme-toggle button in every header; the content container is 1192 px, not 1240 px (everything starts 24 px further right); phone gutters are 16 px, not 24 px.
7. The docs decisions.json block has no file-name header or copy button; the changelog "Latest" pill and the docs severity pills don't stretch the way the design does.
