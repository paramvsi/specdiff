Card

A saved payment card. Returned inside Payment and Customer objects.

  • brandstringrequiredCard network. One of visa, mastercard, amex.
  • exp_monthintegerrequiredExpiry month, 1 to 12.
  • exp_yearintegerrequiredFour-digit expiry year.
  • fingerprintstringoptionalNewStable identifier for the card number. Use it to spot duplicate cards.
  • last4stringBreakingRemoved in 2.0.0. Use fingerprint to identify the card; show the last four digits from your own records.

Used in

  • GET/v1/payments/{id}→ card
  • GET/v1/payments→ data[].card
  • POST/v1/payments→ card (response)
Example
{
  "brand": "visa",
  "exp_month": 4,
  "exp_year": 2029,
  "fingerprint": "Xt5E9kq…"
}
