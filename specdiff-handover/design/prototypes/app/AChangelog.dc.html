## v2.0.0, 14 Nov 2026

### Breaking changes
- Removed `DELETE /v1/refunds/{id}`. Use `POST /v1/refunds/{id}/cancel`. ← edited by you
- `POST /v1/payments` now requires `currency` (ISO 4217).
- Removed `card.last4` from payment responses.

### Worth checking
- `limit` on `GET /v1/payments` now maxes out at 50.
- Payment `status` can now be `disputed`.

### Added
- `POST /v1/payouts`
- `metadata` on payment responses
